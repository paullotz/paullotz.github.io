# Book Notes: Essentialism

### Core Philosophy
* **Essentialism:** Doing less but better ("weniger aber besser").
* **Focus Energy:** Concentrate energy on isolated areas that bring you benefit instead of spreading it thin across 10 different directions.
* **Vital Few vs. Trivial Many:** Focus on the small number of things that actually matter.
* **Ownership:** If you don't prioritize your life, someone else will.

### Decision Making & The "No"
* **The 90% Rule:** Be selective. Ask "Do I really love this?" Only decide if you are 90% sure.
* **Clear Logic:** If it is not a clear "yes," then it is a clear "no."
* **The Trade-off:** Empower a quick "no" and a slow "yes." Recognize that saying "yes" to one opportunity is inherently saying "no" to several others.
* **Social Impact:** Saying "no" may have a short-term social impact on relationships, but respect grows over time.
* **Eliminate the Non-Essential:** Learn to say "no" to useless meetings and tasks to spend time on important work.

### Mindset & Productivity
* **Big Picture:** Stop thinking about minor details and focus on the bigger picture.
* **Play & Creativity:** Play is important for the mind and creativity. Seek playful environments and ask: *"What sparked joy in your childhood?"*
* **Choice:** Move from "I have to" to **"I choose to."**
* **Mental Space:** Block hours per day for "focus mode" and allow yourself to **be bored.**
* **Sunk Cost Bias:** Be aware of the emotional attachment to past investments that no longer serve you.
* **Zero-Based Budgeting:** Don't budget time based on existing commitments; begin from scratch.
* **The Endowment Effect:** Ask: "If I didn't own this item, how much would I pay for it?"

### Execution & Progress
* **Remove Constraints:** Find the "slowest hiker" in your life (the main bottleneck) and remove it.
* **Small Wins:** Focus on small wins and celebrate their progress. Making progress in a meaningful way is the best motivation.
* **Habits:** Find the cue that triggers non-essential behavior.
* **Boundaries:** Write down deal-breakers anytime you feel them violated.
* **Clarity:** Always ask: "How will we know we are done?"
