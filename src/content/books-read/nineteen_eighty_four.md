## Summary & Synopsis
A classic novel depicting life under totalitarian rule, where constant state surveillance, historical distortion, and ideological control enforce complete obedience.

## Key Takeaways & Detailed Notes
- **Language as Thought Control:** Through the systematic reduction and alteration of language (*Newspeak*), the regime limits the range of expressible thought.
- **Historical Revisionism:** The party rewrites records so that the official past matches current policy.
- **Doublethink:** Holding two contradictory beliefs at once and accepting both as true.
