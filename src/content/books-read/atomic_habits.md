## Summary & Synopsis
A practical guide to building habits through incremental adjustments, cue-craving-response-reward feedback loops, and identity shifts.

## Key Takeaways & Detailed Notes

### 1. Compounding & Latent Potential
- **1% Better Every Day:** A 1% improvement each day yields a 37x compounding increase over one year.
- **Plateau of Latent Potential:** Habits take time to produce visible results. Early progress often feels slow before reaching a noticeable turning point.

### 2. Goals vs. Systems
- You do not rise to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your systems.
- Goals define direction; systems determine actual progress.

### 3. Identity-Based Habits
- The most effective habits come from internal identity rather than outcome desires.
  - *"I am trying to quit smoking"* vs. *"I am not a smoker."*
- Decide the type of person you wish to be, and prove it to yourself with small daily actions.

### 4. Implementation Rules
- **The 2-Minute Rule:** When starting a new habit, make sure it takes less than two minutes to begin.
- **Habit Stacking:** Tie a desired new behavior to an established routine (`After [Current Habit], I will [New Habit]`).
- **Instant Gratification vs Long-Term Rewards:** The human brain evolved under immediate-return environments; make good habits immediately satisfying.
- **Never Miss Twice Rule:** Missing once is an accident; missing twice is the start of a new, negative habit. Get back on track the next day.
