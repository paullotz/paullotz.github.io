## Why I Read This
Starker Klassiker: Figurenpsychologie, Spannung, Darstellung von Abhängigkeit und Selbstzerstörung; genau die Art klassischer Literatur, die mich packt.

## Summary & Synopsis
A psychological novella about a young tutor drawn to roulette in a German casino town while dealing with the debts and eccentricities of an aristocratic family.

## Key Takeaways & Detailed Notes
- **Psychological Intensity:** A direct look at the loop of euphoria, compulsion, and impulsive risk.
- **Human Contradiction:** Dostoevsky's sharp focus on pride, shame, and social pretension.
