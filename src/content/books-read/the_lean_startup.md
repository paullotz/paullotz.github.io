## Why I Read This
Validated Learning, Build-Measure-Learn Feedback-Loop und Small-Batch-Entwicklung für SaaS-Produkte.

## Summary & Synopsis
A methodology for testing product hypotheses through quick iteration cycles, early user feedback, and small batches.

## Key Takeaways & Detailed Notes

### 1. Build-Measure-Learn Feedback Loop
- The primary job in an early-stage project is turning ideas into tests, measuring how users actually behave, and deciding whether to pivot or keep going.
- Moving through that feedback cycle quickly matters more than polishing unvalidated features.

### 2. Batch Sizes & Steering
- **Small Batches over Big Releases:** Working in small increments surfaces problems early and reduces wasted effort (like steering a car rather than launching a pre-programmed rocket).
- **Genchi Genbutsu:** Observe real user behavior directly rather than assuming what customers need.
- The objective is finding out what people actually want and will pay for as fast as possible.
